## Civic supply contracts

The city posts standing orders for what it needs: timber, stone, food, materials. Deliver the items and the treasury pays on the spot. Nobody has to approve it.

<InfoCallout type="info" title="Contracts are bonus income, not your main income">
  A contract pays more than dumping the same stack at the admin shop, and less than a good day on the player market. Cooldowns and daily caps stop you living off the board. Use it to top up a slow session, not to fund your week.
</InfoCallout>

### How it works

1. Run `/contracts` to see the board. Rewards, cooldowns, and caps get tuned live, so the in-game board is the source of truth
2. Run `/contractinfo <id>` for the full detail on one contract, including your cooldown and daily count
3. Gather the requested items into your inventory
4. Run `/deliver <id>`. The city takes the items and pays you instantly

Full mechanics on the wiki: [Contracts](https://wiki.prosperitysmp.com/Contracts).

## Government build contracts

Bigger physical builds are a separate track, run by staff, and they're where the [Builder](/skill-trees#builder) career actually makes its money.

Staff open a build contract naming the builder and a protected build site. Inside it you get flight and region-limited building tools, and **the city supplies the materials**. You're paid for hours and skill. A first small commission delivered well is how builders get referred upward, all the way to owner-awarded civic landmarks with pay negotiated case by case.

If you want to build for the city, ask in the [Discord](https://discord.gg/gmXqBx7sfV).

Full details on the wiki: [Government build contracts](https://wiki.prosperitysmp.com/Government_build_contracts).

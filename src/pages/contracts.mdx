## Civic Supply Contracts

The city posts standing contracts for needed resources — timber, stone, food, materials. Bring the requested items, deliver them, and the city treasury pays you on the spot. Fully automated, no staff needed.

<InfoCallout type="info" title="Contracts are bonus income, not your main income">
  Contracts pay above the admin floor — better than dumping to the server — but below player market peaks. Cooldowns and daily caps keep them steady supplementary income rather than a grind loop. Use them between bigger projects, or chain them while leveling.
</InfoCallout>

### How it works

1. Run `/contracts` to see the current board — contracts, rewards, cooldowns, and caps are tuned live, so the in-game board is always the source of truth
2. Run `/contractinfo <id>` for full details on one contract (including your cooldown and daily count)
3. Gather the requested items in your inventory
4. Run `/deliver <id>` — the city takes the items and pays you instantly

Full mechanics on the wiki: [Contracts](https://wiki.prosperitysmp.com/Contracts).

## Government Build Contracts

Bigger physical builds are a separate, staff-run track — and the core of the [Builder](/skill-trees#builder) career's income.

Staff open a build contract naming the builder and a protected build site. Inside the site, the builder gets flight and region-limited building tools, with **materials supplied by the city** — you're paid for skill and time, not resources. Quality work on smaller commissions leads to invitations for bigger ones, up to owner-awarded civic landmarks with negotiated pay.

Want to build for the city? Ask in the [Discord](https://discord.gg/gmXqBx7sfV).

Full details on the wiki: [Government build contracts](https://wiki.prosperitysmp.com/Government_build_contracts).

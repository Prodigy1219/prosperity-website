## Philosophy

The economy is built around **player trade**. The admin shop is a fallback floor, not the main market. High-end items come from players — elytras, enchanted tools, drills, beacons, rare materials.

If you want a diamond pickaxe, you buy it from a player or craft it yourself. If you want a Fortune III tool, you find someone who built the progression to make one.

<InfoCallout type="info" title="Live tuning may occur">
  Economy values, prices, and rewards may be adjusted post-launch based on observed inflation and player feedback. Patches are posted in the [changelog](/changelog). Current per-item figures always live on the [wiki](https://wiki.prosperitysmp.com) and the [economy dashboard](https://economy.prosperitysmp.com).
</InfoCallout>

## How Income Works

Your hourly income is the sum of five sources:

| Source | Description |
|---|---|
| **Job wage** | Direct cash from Jobs Reborn when you take an action (mine a block, harvest a crop, kill a mob) |
| **Admin floor** | Value of your drops sold to the admin shop — the safe fallback, not the real market |
| **Player market** | Extra value from selling to other players — this is the real economy |
| **Job stipends** | Small random bonus drops while doing your career's work (gunpowder for Miners, bone meal for Farmers, etc.) |
| **Contracts** | Controlled bonus payouts from public contracts, civic commissions, and events |

The job wage is intentionally modest. Real income comes from selling what you collect to other players at better prices than the admin floor.

**Target effective income by stage:**

| Stage | Target $/hr |
|---|---|
| New player, hand-mining (level 1) | $375–$500 |
| Mid-level (around level 25) | $500–$750 |
| Level 50 maxed job, hand work | $690–$1,000 |
| Level 50 + T2/T3 drill or harvester | $1,000–$1,750 |
| Prestige 5 + Master Tool | $1,750–$2,500 |

## The Three-Price Rule

Every important item has three prices:

```
Admin buy  <  Player market  <  Admin sell
```

- **Admin buy** — The server buys at 30–50% of player market value. It's a floor, not the real price.
- **Player market** — The true economy value, set by supply and demand.
- **Admin sell** — If the server sells it at all, it's at 150–300% of player market. A convenience tax, not the standard price.

If admin buy prices are too close to player market prices, players stop trading with each other and just dump everything to the server. The spread is intentional.

## Checking Prices

Prices are living numbers — don't trust static tables:

- **`/market <item>`** in game — median price from recent real player trades
- The **[economy dashboard](https://economy.prosperitysmp.com)** — item worth estimates and market data
- The wiki's [Item worth](https://wiki.prosperitysmp.com/Item_worth) and [Admin shop](https://wiki.prosperitysmp.com/Admin_shop) pages — how the pricing systems work

## Money Faucets

Money enters the economy through:

- **Jobs** — Regular wage paid per manual action in your profession
- **Public contracts** — The contract board pays for needed resources
- **Job stipends** — Random bonus drops while doing your career's work (see the [wiki's Stipends page](https://wiki.prosperitysmp.com/Stipends) for current rates)
- **Admin shop sales** — Selling basic raw materials at floor prices
- **Daily & playtime rewards** — `/daily` login streaks and `/rewards` playtime chests

## Money Sinks

Money leaves the economy through:

- **Plot rental** — Weekly fees for rented apartments, plots, market stalls
- **Tools and equipment** — Drills, harvesters, enchanted gear (tools wear out and must be replaced)
- **Repair costs** — Anvil repair with matching ingots; no Mending in normal play
- **Master Smith books** — high-tier enchant books ($10,000–$120,000 plus diamond blocks)
- **Fines** — Law violations result in fines paid to the city or victims
- **Prestige** — The biggest late-game sink — see below

## Banks & Markets

Beyond shops, four live systems move money around:

- **The Central Bank** is open — flexible savings, 7-day bonds, and **stocks** (the Prosperity Index), with a full trading desk at [economy.prosperitysmp.com/stocks.html](https://economy.prosperitysmp.com/stocks.html). See the wiki's [Banking](https://wiki.prosperitysmp.com/Banking) and [Stock market](https://wiki.prosperitysmp.com/Stock_market) pages.
- **The Auction House** (`/ah`) — server-wide item marketplace with listing fees and a sale cut. Listing is live; the buy path is still being fixed, so treat it as in beta for now. Wiki: [Auction house](https://wiki.prosperitysmp.com/Auction_house).
- **The Casino** — coinflip, crash, slots, and a daily lottery. Wiki: [Casino](https://wiki.prosperitysmp.com/Casino).
- **The Bazaar** — the admin shop's decorative/catalog section. Wiki: [Bazaar](https://wiki.prosperitysmp.com/Bazaar).

## Jobs and Progression

Jobs are the core progression system. Join up to 3 jobs, level each to 50, and unlock perks through milestones as you go.

| Stat | Value |
|---|---|
| Max jobs at once | 3 (max for Season 0) |
| Max level per job | 50 |
| Income bonus | +1% per level above level 1, max +49% at level 50 |
| Time to max | ~50 hours of active work |

As you level up, your job title advances — Novice → Apprentice → Elite → Master → Grand Master → Legendary (Hunter uses combat ranks E→S instead). Job-specific milestones unlock real progression items at certain levels — see each job's page on the [wiki](https://wiki.prosperitysmp.com/Jobs).

## Drills and Harvesters

3×3 AOE mining and farming tools, bought with in-game money (`/buydrill`, `/buyharvester`) and gated by Miner/Farmer level (tiers at L15/L30/L45). They're repairable via anvil + matching ingot, and eventually become too expensive to repair — that's the long-term sink. Current prices and specs: wiki [Drills and harvesters](https://wiki.prosperitysmp.com/Drills_and_harvesters).

## Prestige System

When you hit level 50 in a job, you can **Prestige** — reset your level back to 1 in exchange for permanent passive bonuses (+5% income / +10% XP per tier, stacking to +25% / +50% at P5) and a shot at a unique Master Tool. Cash costs run $10,000 (P1) to $400,000 (P5) per job, plus materials — the largest single sink in the game. Details: [Skill Trees & Jobs](/skill-trees#prestige) and the wiki's [Prestige](https://wiki.prosperitysmp.com/Prestige) page.

## Admin Shop Rules

The admin shop is a **price floor**, not a store. It exists so players always have a guaranteed fallback buyer.

**The server buys:** Basic raw materials — stone, logs, basic crops, raw ores, coal, wool — at floor prices that stay well below player market.

**The server sells (fallback only, expensive):** Basic tools, food, torches, seeds — priced well above player market so player shops always win.

**The server never sells:** Diamonds, emeralds, netherite, ancient debris, enchanted items, elytra, beacons, totems, rare drops, music discs, mob heads, dragon eggs.

## Player Shops

Player shops are the primary market:

- Any player can open a shop using a ChestShop sign in any plot or market stall they rent
- Shop owners set their own prices — supply and demand drive the market
- Use `/find <item>` to search active ChestShops on the server
- Player shops typically beat admin shop floor prices on common goods

## Land as Wealth

Land is one of the most important assets on the server. Plots come in three zones — Residential, Commercial, Industrial — plus apartments and market stalls. **Rentals are open** (apartments, stalls, shops); **commercial plot purchases are paused until the new map**. Renting and buying happen through the plot signs — see the wiki's [Real estate](https://wiki.prosperitysmp.com/Real_estate) and [Plot rental](https://wiki.prosperitysmp.com/Plot_rental) pages.

Owning land is a milestone. It's a home, a store of value, and a business foundation.

## Public Contracts

Public contracts bridge player productivity and world development:

- The city posts contracts for needed resources: timber, stone, food, materials
- Players complete contracts and are paid by the city treasury — see the [contracts page](/contracts)
- Resources are consumed (sink), money is paid out (faucet) but always less than market value
- This is a steady money faucet for active, organized players, capped by daily limits to prevent farming

## Inflation Control

The economy has built-in protections:

- **Monthly review:** Jobs wages tested and adjusted if combined income is too high or too low
- **Admin shop spread:** Buy prices stay well below player market prices
- **Plot rental:** Continuous money sink for every active player
- **Tool wear:** No Mending in normal play means tools must be repaired with materials or rebought; both are sinks
- **Prestige cost:** The largest single sink in the game ($10k–$400k per tier)

## Boosted Weekends

Periodically, the server runs **boosted weekends** — Jobs Reborn wages and XP get a **1.5× multiplier** for the weekend. They're announced in [Discord](https://discord.gg/gmXqBx7sfV) ahead of time — a great window to grind milestones, save for a big purchase, or push toward a prestige tier.

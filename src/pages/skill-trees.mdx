## Overview

ProsperitySMP has two connected progression systems: **Jobs** and **Skill Trees**.

- **Jobs** earn wages, have levels (1–50), and grant titles as you level up. Join via `/jobs join`.
- **Skill Trees** are a live perk system tied to your job level. Open the GUI with `/skills` to see and unlock your job's nodes.

You do not need to buy anything to unlock skill nodes — they cost skill points earned by leveling your job. For the current node list and effects per job, see each job's page on the [wiki](https://wiki.prosperitysmp.com/Skill_trees).

## Job Slots

| Slot | Cost |
|---|---|
| Slots 1–3 | Free |

Each player can hold **3 jobs at once** in Season 0. Additional slots may unlock in a future season.

## Specialization vs Diversification

Both paths are valid — the system is designed so neither feels wrong.

| Path | What You Get |
|---|---|
| **Specialize** (go deep in one job) | Stronger wage scaling, better titles, better contracts, stronger skill perks |
| **Diversify** (level multiple jobs) | More business models, more income sources, more flexibility |

A level 50 Miner is the best miner on the server. A player with Miner + Builder + Woodcutter has more options. Pick based on how you want to play.

## The 6 Jobs

Each job has 50 levels, takes ~50 hours to max, and grants a +1% income bonus per level (max +50% at level 50, applied to job wage). Five of the six jobs share the same title ladder:

| Level | Title |
|---|---|
| 1–9 | Novice |
| 10–19 | Apprentice |
| 20–29 | Elite |
| 30–39 | Master |
| 40–49 | Grand Master |
| 50 | Legendary |

**Hunter is the exception** — it uses combat ranks **E → S** instead (see below).

### Miner

Resource extraction, underground operations, high-tier tools. Signature perks include underground night vision, auto-smelting, and bulk ore compression — and Miner levels gate the **drills**: 3×3 AOE mining pickaxes bought with in-game money (`/buydrill`). Full node list and drill tiers: wiki [Miner](https://wiki.prosperitysmp.com/Miner) and [Drills and harvesters](https://wiki.prosperitysmp.com/Drills_and_harvesters).

### Farmer

Food production, ranching, and bulk harvest. Breed and tend animals for money, craft signature consumables at high level, and unlock the **harvesters**: 3×3 auto-replanting AOE hoes (`/buyharvester`). Full details: wiki [Farmer](https://wiki.prosperitysmp.com/Farmer) and [Drills and harvesters](https://wiki.prosperitysmp.com/Drills_and_harvesters).

### Builder

Construction, public works, architectural projects — and the highest-paying commissions on the server for players with real building skill.

If you love building, this is your job. The economy is structured so that **skilled builders are paid like specialists, not laborers** — routine block-placement wages are modest by design, and the real income comes from contracts. Builder isn't a grind-to-50 job; **it's an audition**: do quality work and bigger city commissions come to you, up to owner-awarded government builds with negotiated pay and materials supplied. Plenty of builders also take **private commissions** from other players — house builds, shop fronts, custom work — at rates the player market sets. See the wiki's [Builder](https://wiki.prosperitysmp.com/Builder) and [Government build contracts](https://wiki.prosperitysmp.com/Government_build_contracts) pages.

**Practical tip:** Builder pairs well as a second or third job — most successful builders carry it alongside Miner or Woodcutter for cheap material supply.

### Woodcutter

Lumber supply, forestry, tree operations. Signature perks include whole-trunk felling, paid log-stripping and charcoal work, and craftable reinforced shields at high level. Full details: wiki [Woodcutter](https://wiki.prosperitysmp.com/Woodcutter).

### Hunter

Dungeon clearing, combat, mob drops, bounties. Hunter uses combat **ranks** instead of titles — clear instanced dungeons for cash bounties, loot crates, and rank XP:

| Rank | Hunter level |
|---|---|
| E | 1 |
| D | 10 |
| C | 20 |
| B | 30 |
| A | 40 |
| S | 50 |

See the wiki's [Hunter](https://wiki.prosperitysmp.com/Hunter) and [Dungeons](https://wiki.prosperitysmp.com/Dungeons) pages.

### Fisherman

Food supply, rare catches, and the custom fish system. Perks improve wages, rod durability, and catch rarity — rare-catch upgrades work with the custom-fish system in the resource world. Full details: wiki [Fisherman](https://wiki.prosperitysmp.com/Fisherman).

## Prestige

When you reach level 50 in a job, you can **Prestige** — your level resets to 1 in exchange for permanent passive bonuses and a shot at a unique **Master Tool** for that career.

Prestige is the largest single money sink in the game. Each tier costs significantly more than the last, and you can prestige independently in every job.

### Prestige Costs

| Tier | Cash cost (per tier, per job) | Reward |
|---|---|---|
| **P1** | $25,000 + materials | +5% income, +10% XP |
| **P2** | $75,000 + materials | +10% income (cumulative), +20% XP |
| **P3** | $200,000 + materials | +15% income, +30% XP |
| **P4** | $500,000 + materials | +20% income, +40% XP |
| **P5** | $1,000,000 + materials | +25% income, +50% XP |

Material requirements per tier are listed on the wiki's [Prestige](https://wiki.prosperitysmp.com/Prestige) page.

### Cumulative Bonuses

| Prestige Level | Total Income Bonus | Total XP Rate |
|---|---|---|
| Just L50 (P0) | +50% | +0% |
| P1 | +55% | +10% |
| P2 | +60% | +20% |
| P3 | +65% | +30% |
| P4 | +70% | +40% |
| P5 | +75% | +50% |

A P5 maxed Miner has +75% income on every block they break, on top of all their tool effects.

### Prestige rewards

Each prestige grants a named **prestige tool** for that career, and prestige crates can roll rarer rewards — including the **Master Tools** (the only source of Mending on the server) and [vouchers](https://wiki.prosperitysmp.com/Vouchers). Full reward details: wiki [Prestige](https://wiki.prosperitysmp.com/Prestige) and [Crates](https://wiki.prosperitysmp.com/Crates).

### Why Prestige Matters

- **Long-term progression** — even after you max a job, there's a next mountain to climb
- **Biggest sink in the economy** — the cash cost of P5 in one job is ~$1.8M, plus major material requirements
- **Prestige is forever** — rewards persist across the season, and cumulative bonuses are kept indefinitely
- **Each job is independent** — you can be P3 Miner with no prestige in other jobs, or P1 in five jobs at once
